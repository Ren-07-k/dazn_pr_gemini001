# Group Stage Data for 2026 FIFA World Cup

## Tournament Format
The 2026 FIFA World Cup will feature an expanded tournament format with 48 teams participating. The teams will be split into 16 groups of 3 teams each. The top 2 teams from each group will advance to the knockout stage.

## Group Analysis
Each team plays the other two teams in their group once, with a total of 3 matches per group. The points are allocated as follows:
- Win: 3 points
- Draw: 1 point
- Loss: 0 points

After all matches, the teams will be ranked by points, goal difference, and goals scored to determine the top two teams that will proceed to the knockout stage.
